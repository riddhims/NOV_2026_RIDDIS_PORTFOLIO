GA4 & Looker Studio

I work with **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to analyze website traffic, user behavior, engagement, and conversion data. I use **Looker Studio** to turn this data into interactive dashboards that make key performance indicators and trends easy to understand. My work involves connecting GA4 data, selecting relevant KPIs, creating visualizations and filters, and presenting insights in a clear and actionable way. This allows me to transform raw analytics data into meaningful reports that support better digital and business decisions.
